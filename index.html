Who's actually
the smartest in the room?

zcas ranked pulls together every CA list we can get our hands on for the January 2025 intake and turns them into one standing per student — a live, honest leaderboard of academic performance across OOP, Databases, Emerging Trends in ICT, IoT, OS Concepts, Academic Writing, Engineering Mathematics, and Professional Ethics in Computing.

01

Every CA, one place

Each course's CA list is imported as-is and ranked on its own terms — your placing in OOP is compared only against everyone else who sat OOP.

02

A fair average

All eight CAs are marked out of 40 (Professional Ethics was originally out of 100, so we scaled it ×0.4 to match), so we can average a student's raw scores across whatever courses they sat, and rank that average against peers who sat the same number of courses.

03

Top %, not just a score

Every result is shown as a "Top X%" — a single, comparable figure for how a student stacks up, whether they sat one course or all eight.
